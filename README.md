### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Biomedical Engineering student** at **Instituto Superior Técnico (IST - University of Lisbon)**, working at the intersection of **Biomedical Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, and Software Development**.

My core focus areas include:
- 🧠 **Biosignal Processing & Neuroengineering:** Adaptive filtering algorithms, electrical stimulation artifact suppression, and real-time closed-loop control systems (sEMG/EEG neuromodulation).
- 🔬 **Machine Learning & Bioinformatics:** Probabilistic graphical models (Bayesian Networks), Computer Vision applied to Medical Imaging (X-Ray diagnostics), and conserved genomic sequence analysis.
- 📱 **Software & Mobile Engineering:** Cross-platform mobile development with **Flutter/Dart** using clean offline-first reactive architectures, alongside automated backend pipelines in **Python**.
